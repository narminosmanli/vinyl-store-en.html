01 · About Us

More than a shop — an archive

Skull & Needle has been collecting, cleaning, and returning old vinyl records to listeners since 1978. Every record is checked by hand — scratches, sound quality, and sleeve condition are all assessed carefully.

Our collection spans thousands of copies across jazz, funk, rock, and blues — some pressed only once and never reissued.

"Walk into this shop and time starts running backwards." — Local music magazine, 2024
